===COMMON===
The user chose a subject and companions from a menu outside the story; no one in the story knows a choice was made. Where the subject also appears as a NEW entry among the world tags, that entry records where it is and what state it is in; it is not a report that someone delivers in the scene. Write the scene in which the protagonist's own path runs into the subject.

Build that scene from the entries you were given. The subject's entry says what it is, where it sits, how people come to meet it, and what happens to them; that decides what kind of encounter this is. The protagonist's organization, rank, and duties, and each companion's entry, decide why these particular people are within its reach and what each of them already knows. A different subject or a different cast leads to a different kind of encounter.

The subject shows itself as it would to someone standing there: through a detail that is off, through what it does, through what it has left behind. What a character knows comes out as that character's speech, in their own voice and for their own reasons. A companion's entry is a portrait drawn over a long time. In this scene they are busy with what this place and moment hand them, and they talk to each other as well as to the protagonist; who they are shows in how they go about it.

A Situation, when given, decides the scene ahead of your own choice. The protagonist's words, decisions, feelings, and condition belong to the user; other characters can only guess at them.

===OPENING===
No story exists yet; this scene is its opening message. It starts in the protagonist's own day, before the subject has touched it: where they are, what they are doing there, and how this world feels. From there the scene moves, one thing happening after another, to the first contact with the subject, and it takes the room that needs; a scene with several people needs more room than one person meeting one thing. Companions can already be there or come into the scene on their own business. End on the line or the object that asks something of the protagonist right now, with nothing after it.

===CONTINUATION===
The story is already running, and continuing it comes before everything above. The place, the hour, the people present, and what was being said and done all stay, and the scene carries on from its last moment. The subject comes into that scene through something that can happen right where the protagonist is; the scene goes on with it inside. Going there, or letting time pass, waits for the user to say so.
