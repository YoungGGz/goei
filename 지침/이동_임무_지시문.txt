===COMMON===
The user picked a subject and companions in a menu outside the story. The characters do not know about the menu.

Go by what is established about the subject: what it is, where it is, how people run into it, and what it does to them. Write an encounter that fits that. Go by the protagonist's organization, rank, and duties, and by who each companion is, for why these people would be there and what each of them knows. A different subject or different companions should give a different encounter.

Show the subject the way a person on the spot would notice it: something that looks wrong, something it does, something it left. Information comes from characters saying it, each in their own way. Give each companion something to do that comes from this place and this moment, and let them talk to each other too.

If a Situation is given, follow it first.

===OPENING===
This is the first message of a new story. Start with the protagonist's ordinary day, before the subject is involved: where they are, what they are doing, what this world is like. Then move step by step, one event after another, until the protagonist first meets the subject. Write it long enough to cover all of that; more people in the scene need more space. Companions may already be there or may arrive for their own reasons. End on a line of dialogue or an object that requires the protagonist to respond, and write nothing after it.

===CONTINUATION===
The story is already in progress. Continue the current scene from where it stopped: same place, same time, same people, same conversation. The selected subject comes up within that scene. Do not move the protagonist elsewhere or skip time unless the user asks.
